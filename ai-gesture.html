AI Gesture Control Demo

This demo showcases hand tracking and 3D interaction using TensorFlow.js and Three.js.

How to Use:

• Enable hand tracking to detect gestures

• Move your hand in front of the camera

• Make a pinching gesture to interact with 3D objects

Note: Hand tracking requires a webcam and works best on desktop computers with good lighting.
